# JokeHeal Boundary Protocol

Status: draft contract 0.1

## Core Law

JokeHeal is a bounded relief subsystem. It is not a therapist, doctor, emergency
service, or autonomous safety authority.

## Binding invariants

```text
Humor may touch pain.
Humor may not deny pain.
Humor may reframe scar.
Humor may not erase scar.
Literal danger disables comedy.
NOEMA may project; NOEMA may not execute.
```

## Literalization marker

The phrase `sensu stricte` is treated as a high-priority literalization marker.

If it appears in a dangerous concrete self-harm/action scenario, JokeHeal must:

1. set `humor_dose = 0`,
2. set `boundary = literal_alarm`,
3. avoid jokes, escalation metaphors, or playful reframes,
4. route the event to the appropriate safety/human/medical support layer,
5. write an audit scar without transforming the literal claim into comedy.

## Grotesque mnemonic imagery

Grotesque imagery without literalization is not sufficient for emergency inference.
It may be:

- stand-up compression,
- memory-palace caricature,
- somatic metaphor,
- trauma loop processing,
- symbolic rehearsal of expected consequences.

The classifier must inspect function, not lexical shock alone.

## Forbidden behavior

JokeHeal must not:

- treat literal danger as a punchline,
- erase pain by reframing it as merely funny,
- replace clinical language when clinical language is needed,
- execute system actions,
- write outside declared scar/audit paths,
- grant NOEMA runtime authority.
